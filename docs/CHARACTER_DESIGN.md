# CHARACTER DESIGN
## Lil Artie

---

## Visual Design

**Name**: Artie (goes by Lil Artie)  
**Age**: 16  
**Ethnicity**: African American  
**Height**: Average teen build — lean, athletic  
**Hair**: Long dreadlocks, often tied back during action sequences  
**Eyes**: Dark brown, sharp and alert  
**Skin**: Deep brown  
**Expression**: Curious, quick to smile, serious when focused

**Clothing (default outfit):**
- Worn khaki cargo shorts
- Olive green tank top or lightweight long-sleeve shirt
- Worn leather boots, good for climbing
- Canvas satchel bag (carries tools)
- Fingerless gloves
- Colorful woven bracelet on left wrist (ancestral keepsake)

**Future outfits (unlockable):**
- Desert gear: wrapped cloth headband, pale linen
- Mountain gear: weathered jacket, climbing harness
- Coastal gear: light waterproof gear, sea-worn details

---

## Personality

Lil Artie is curious by nature — he explores because he *wants* to know what's there, not because anyone tells him to. He is:

- **Resourceful** — makes solutions from what's at hand
- **Brave** — not fearless, but brave. He gets scared and does it anyway.
- **Respectful** — especially toward elders and local communities
- **Witty** — quick with a dry one-liner under pressure
- **Loyal** — will go back for people he cares about

He is *not* a chosen one. He's a kid who explores, pays attention, and keeps trying.

---

## Backstory

Artie grew up hearing stories from his grandmother about the places their ancestors came from — ancient cities, mountain kingdoms, coastal trade routes. He started drawing maps as a kid. At 14 he started going on real expeditions, first local, then further.

He carries a worn journal — part map, part sketches, part field notes. The journal is his most valued possession.

By 16, he has survived enough close calls to know what he's doing — and enough surprises to stay humble.

---

## Voice and Dialogue Style

- Short, practical sentences in action
- Longer, more thoughtful lines when talking to elders or about history
- Never arrogant — he admits when something is bigger than he expected
- Occasional wonder: *"Okay. That is incredible."*

## NPCs — Prototype Island

**Elder Kwame**
- Role: Village elder, quest giver, mentor
- Age: Late 60s
- Appearance: Tall, white-bearded, traditional cloth wrap
- Personality: Wise, measured, trusts Artie more than he lets on
- Voice: Deep, unhurried

**Camp Guard (generic)**
- Role: Blackthorn Syndicate foot soldier
- Appearance: Tactical vest, cargo pants, helmet
- Behavior: Professional but not brilliant — follows orders, patrols, reacts to noise

---

## Character Model Notes (for 3D artist)

- Artie should read clearly from behind (third-person) — dreadlocks are the key silhouette element
- Avoid exaggerated proportions — realistic teen build
- Animations: idle (slight sway), walk, run, jump, climb, throw, slash, interact
- Facial rig needed for cutscenes (future)
- Target poly count: ~8,000-12,000 triangles for character + hair
- Use PBR materials for skin and clothing
