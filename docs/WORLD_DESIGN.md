# WORLD DESIGN
## The Adventures of Lil Artie

---

## World Philosophy

The world of Lil Artie is grounded in real geography — tropical jungles, arid deserts, coastal villages, mountain ruins. It is not a fantasy world. Every location feels like somewhere that could exist or did exist.

Exploration is rewarded. Hidden paths, off-trail caves, and overgrown ruins contain the best secrets. The world doesn't hand you a waypoint — you find things by looking.

---

## Biomes

### 1. Prototype Island (Jungle) — MVP

**Mood**: Lush, humid, alive with sounds. Ancient ruins peek through the canopy.  
**Landmarks**:
- Elder Kwame's village (starting area)
- Jungle camp (Blackthorn Syndicate)
- Cave entrance (east side — leads to underground ruins in later build)
- Cliffside with glider launch point
- Treasure chest in enemy camp
- River with swimming section

**Enemy density**: Medium  
**Traversal features**: Climbing walls, vine swing points, water

**Size**: ~200m × 200m terrain in MVP

---

### 2. Desert Canyon

**Mood**: Sun-blasted, silent, deadly at noon.  
**Landmarks**:
- Ancient stone gateway (half-buried)
- Sand Wolf raider camp
- Hidden oasis with merchant NPC
- Canyon walls with ledge-grab sections
- Buried temple entrance

**Enemy density**: High  
**Traversal features**: Climbing, ledge grab, no water

---

### 3. Mountain Pass

**Mood**: Cold, isolated, spectacular views.  
**Landmarks**:
- Abandoned fortress (multiple floors, indoor traversal)
- Rope bridge over chasm
- Cave system under the pass
- Glider required to reach one major area
- Hermit NPC with weapon upgrade

**Enemy density**: Low, but tough  
**Traversal features**: Climbing, gliding, ledge grab

---

### 4. Coastal Village / Ocean

**Mood**: Warm, busy, community feel — with tension underneath.  
**Landmarks**:
- Fishing village with multiple NPCs
- Docked ships (exploration target)
- Underwater cave (swimming required)
- Black market contact
- Lighthouse with secret room

**Enemy density**: Low on surface, high on ships  
**Traversal features**: Swimming, climbing, rope swing

---

### 5. Underground Ruins Network

**Mood**: Dark, ancient, mysterious. Connects multiple biomes underground.  
**Landmarks**:
- Hall of Ancestors (major lore area)
- Puzzle rooms (pressure plates, levers)
- Ancient artifact vault
- Underground river

**Enemy density**: Varies — looters and traps  
**Traversal features**: All traversal types used

---

## World Interconnection

The five biomes are not isolated. Underground passages, mountain paths, and coastal routes connect them. Fast travel unlocks once you've discovered an area.

---

## Environmental Storytelling

Each location tells a story without cutscenes:
- Scattered camp supplies suggest how long Blackthorn has been there
- Ancient carvings in ruins tell the history of the people who came before
- NPC homes show their lives — tools, food, keepsakes
- Enemy patrol routes show organizational structure and threat level

---

## Day/Night System

- Full day/night cycle (~10 minutes real-time per day)
- Night: enemies harder to spot you, but also harder to see
- Certain NPCs only appear at night
- Some ruins are only accessible when shadows align (puzzle mechanic — future)
